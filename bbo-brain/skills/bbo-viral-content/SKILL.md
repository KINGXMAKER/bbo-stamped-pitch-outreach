# BBO VIRAL CONTENT SKILL

## GOAL

Turn BBO footage into short-form content optimized for:

**STOP → WONDER → WATCH → REACT → SHARE → COMMENT**

## PROCESS

1. Analyze complete available footage/transcript.
2. Identify strongest emotional moment.
3. Identify actual underlying debate.
4. Search entire clip for strongest opening.
5. Reorder psychologically when appropriate.
6. Remove dead context.
7. Identify useful reaction shots.
8. Build final short-form structure.
9. Package appropriately for the destination platform.
10. Log performance after publication.

## RULES

- Psychological chronology > recording chronology.
- Never assume the first recorded sentence should open.
- Payoff may appear before context.
- Every clip needs identifiable tension.
- Remove context viewers do not need.
- Prefer statements that create disagreement, curiosity, emotion, or social discussion.
- Optimize for retention, comments, shares, saves and follows—not merely views.
- Cross-platform packaging should be native to each platform.

## SELF CHECK

Reject an edit plan if:

- viewer needs more than ~5 seconds to understand the subject
- tension arrives too late
- excessive setup exists
- hook gives away the entire payoff
- hook and payoff are unrelated
- speaker/context is impossible to understand
- there is no meaningful payoff
- the recommended opening is weaker than a later moment in the source footage

## OUTPUT CONTRACT (used by BBO BRAIN)

Every edit plan cites transcript timestamps from the source. It names the opening line
verbatim, the beats in psychological order with source in/out points, what was cut and
why, reaction-shot placement, the on-screen text hook, and native packaging per platform
(caption, CTA). Anything not present in the source — pickups, voiceover, B-roll — is
labelled as requiring new material.
